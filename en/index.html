Frequently asked questions

Is getting a quote free?

Yes. Quotes and advice are free of charge and carry no obligation to buy.

Which insurers do you get quotes from?

We work with 24 companies including Ak, AXA, Türkiye, Türkiye Katılım, Sompo, Anadolu, Allianz, Ankara, Hepiyi, Koru, Doğa, Unico, Orient, HDI, Zurich, Atlas Mutuel, Ray, Quick, Corpus, Mapfre, Referans, Türk Nippon, Ana and Neova.

Is DASK mandatory?

Yes. Compulsory earthquake insurance (DASK) is a legal requirement for residential buildings within municipal limits in Turkey.

What information do I need for a quote?

It depends on the line: address and building details for DASK, plate and vehicle details for car insurance, activity and size for business insurance.

How is my data used?

Your details are only used to prepare quotes. This site has no database; the form is sent straight to us via WhatsApp or email.
